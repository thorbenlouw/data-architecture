Powered by Azure OpenAI and integrated with Microsoft Foundry, it is included at no extra cost with SAS Viya and is switched on by an administrator. Assistants are added per application: code assistance in [SAS Data and AI Studio](sas-data-and-ai-studio.md) and a Visual Studio Code extension, and assistants in [SAS Model Studio](sas-model-studio.md), [SAS Model Manager](sas-model-manager.md) and [SAS Intelligent Decisioning](sas-intelligent-decisioning.md). SAS also describes data discovery in [SAS Data Governance](sas-data-governance.md) and plans dashboard creation in [SAS Visual Analytics](sas-visual-analytics.md). Industry copilots for asset and liability management and clinical data discovery are available, and SAS plans more during 2026.
