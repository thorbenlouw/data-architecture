Runs on Azure, AWS, Google Cloud, Red Hat OpenShift or on premises, managed by the customer or by SAS. Sold in four editions (SAS Viya, SAS Viya Advanced, SAS Viya Enterprise and SAS Viya Programming): Advanced adds forecasting, text analytics, optimisation and econometrics, Enterprise adds decisions, streaming analytics and in-database acceleration, and Programming covers code-based work without model management or data governance. Stable releases ship monthly, named by year and month such as 2026.10, and Long-Term Support releases every six months.
