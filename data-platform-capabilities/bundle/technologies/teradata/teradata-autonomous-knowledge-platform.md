Renamed from Teradata Vantage in May 2026. Teradata describes six layers: data foundation, knowledge context, AI, agent execution, agents and workspaces. The same software runs as [Teradata Cloud](teradata-cloud.md) and [Teradata Factory](teradata-factory.md), so cloud, on-premises and hybrid estates are possible.
