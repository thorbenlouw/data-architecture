# Technologies by platform

* [Amazon Web Services](aws/) - 113 technologies. Amazon's cloud, with separate managed services for each part of the data and AI stack.
* [Microsoft Azure and Fabric](microsoft/) - 139 technologies. Microsoft's Azure services plus Fabric, its SaaS analytics platform, together with Power BI and Purview.
* [Google Cloud](google-cloud/) - 132 technologies. Google's cloud, centred on BigQuery for analytics and its agent platform for AI.
* [Snowflake](snowflake/) - 126 technologies. A multi-cloud SaaS data platform built around a managed SQL engine, with engineering, AI and sharing services on top.
* [Databricks](databricks/) - 90 technologies. A multi-cloud data and AI platform built on Apache Spark, Delta Lake and Unity Catalog.
* [ClickHouse](clickhouse/) - 49 technologies. An open-source columnar database for real-time analytics, also sold as a managed cloud service.
* [Informatica](informatica/) - 45 technologies. An enterprise data management suite for integration, quality, governance, MDM and privacy, now owned by Salesforce.
* [Cloudera](cloudera/) - 25 technologies. A hybrid data platform for on-premises and cloud deployments that grew out of the Hadoop ecosystem.
* [Oracle](oracle/) - 25 technologies. Oracle's database-centred data and AI services on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure and other clouds.
* [SAP](sap/) - 26 technologies. SAP's data and analytics services for data from SAP business applications and other sources.
* [Teradata](teradata/) - 18 technologies. An enterprise analytics database and platform for large-scale warehousing, on premises and in the cloud.
* [IBM](ibm/) - 27 technologies. IBM's watsonx data, AI and governance products, with integration tools and Confluent streaming.
* [Palantir](palantir/) - 25 technologies. A platform that models enterprise data as an operational ontology for analytics, applications and AI agents.
* [Open-source ecosystem](open-source/) - 119 technologies. Open-source projects and open standards that underpin or complement commercial platforms.
* [Independent vendors](independent/) - 131 technologies. Commercial tools from vendors that work across platforms rather than offering a complete platform.
