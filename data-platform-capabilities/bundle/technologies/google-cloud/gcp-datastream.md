Replicates directly into BigQuery with automatic schema handling and backfill, or into Iceberg tables. Workday, ServiceNow, Salesforce Marketing Cloud and Microsoft Dataverse sources entered preview in July 2026. The first 100 GiB per month of CDC from Google Cloud sources has been free since June 2026.

# Realises

- [Change data capture](../../capabilities/ingestion-integration/change-data-capture.md) (Ingestion and integration) (primary): Capture inserts, updates and deletes from source databases, usually from transaction logs, and apply them downstream with low latency.
- [Database replication and mirroring](../../capabilities/ingestion-integration/database-replication.md) (Ingestion and integration): Replicate operational databases continuously into the analytical platform without building pipelines.
- [SaaS application connectors](../../capabilities/ingestion-integration/saas-connectors.md) (Ingestion and integration): Pull data from business applications such as CRM, ERP and marketing tools through prebuilt connectors.
