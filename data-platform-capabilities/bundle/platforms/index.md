# Platforms

* [Amazon Web Services](platform-aws.md) - Amazon's cloud, with separate managed services for each part of the data and AI stack.
* [Microsoft Azure and Fabric](platform-microsoft.md) - Microsoft's Azure services plus Fabric, its SaaS analytics platform, together with Power BI and Purview.
* [Google Cloud](platform-google-cloud.md) - Google's cloud, centred on BigQuery for analytics and its agent platform for AI.
* [Snowflake](platform-snowflake.md) - A multi-cloud SaaS data platform built around a managed SQL engine, with engineering, AI and sharing services on top.
* [Databricks](platform-databricks.md) - A multi-cloud data and AI platform built on Apache Spark, Delta Lake and Unity Catalog.
* [ClickHouse](platform-clickhouse.md) - An open-source columnar database for real-time analytics, also sold as a managed cloud service.
* [Informatica](platform-informatica.md) - An enterprise data management suite for integration, quality, governance, MDM and privacy, now owned by Salesforce.
* [Cloudera](platform-cloudera.md) - A hybrid data platform for on-premises and cloud deployments that grew out of the Hadoop ecosystem.
* [Oracle](platform-oracle.md) - Oracle's database-centred data and AI services on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure and other clouds.
* [SAP](platform-sap.md) - SAP's data and analytics services for data from SAP business applications and other sources.
* [Teradata](platform-teradata.md) - An enterprise analytics database and platform for large-scale warehousing, on premises and in the cloud.
* [IBM](platform-ibm.md) - IBM's watsonx data, AI and governance products, with integration tools and Confluent streaming.
* [Palantir](platform-palantir.md) - A platform that models enterprise data as an operational ontology for analytics, applications and AI agents.
* [SAS](platform-sas.md) - An analytics and AI platform, SAS Viya, for preparing and governing data, building and managing models, and running automated decisions, alongside the long-standing SAS 9 software.
* [Dataiku](platform-dataiku.md) - A platform for building data pipelines, machine learning models, generative AI applications and AI agents visually or in code, on top of an organisation's existing data platforms.
* [Open-source ecosystem](platform-open-source.md) - Open-source projects and open standards that underpin or complement commercial platforms.
* [Independent vendors](platform-independent.md) - Commercial tools from vendors that work across platforms rather than offering a complete platform.
