# Data sharing and collaboration

* [Data sharing and collaboration](data-sharing.md) - Share data within and beyond the organisation safely, without unnecessary copies.
  * [Data application distribution](application-sharing.md) - Package code, models, interfaces and data as installable applications that run in the consumer's environment.
  * [Data clean rooms](data-clean-rooms.md) - Let organisations combine and analyse data jointly while each keeps control over what the others can see.
  * [Data marketplace](data-marketplace.md) - Publish, find and subscribe to data products and datasets, internally or commercially.
  * [External data sharing](external-data-sharing.md) - Share live data with customers, partners and suppliers under agreed terms.
    * [Cross-region and cross-cloud sharing](cross-region-sharing.md) - Share data with consumers on other clouds or regions, replicating it where needed.
    * [Open-protocol sharing](open-protocol-sharing.md) - Share live tables with consumers on other platforms through open protocols, so they need not use the same vendor.
  * [Internal data sharing](internal-data-sharing.md) - Share live data between teams, accounts and workspaces without copying it.
