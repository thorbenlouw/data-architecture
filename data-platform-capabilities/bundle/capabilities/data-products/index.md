# Data products

* [Data products](data-products.md) - Manage data as products with owners, consumers, contracts and service levels.
  * [Data contracts](data-contracts.md) - Agree and enforce a dataset's schema, meaning, quality and service levels between producers and consumers.
  * [Data product lifecycle management](data-product-lifecycle.md) - Version, publish, deprecate and retire data products with notice to their consumers.
  * [Data product specification](data-product-specification.md) - Describe a data product's purpose, outputs, owners and terms of use in a standard, machine-readable form.
  * [Data product usage metering](data-product-metering.md) - Measure how much each consumer uses a data product so it can be billed, charged back or capped.
  * [Data service levels](data-slas.md) - Set and track service levels for data products, such as freshness, availability and quality.
  * [Vendor-supplied application data products](application-data-products.md) - Use governed, documented datasets that business application vendors publish and maintain, instead of building extraction.
