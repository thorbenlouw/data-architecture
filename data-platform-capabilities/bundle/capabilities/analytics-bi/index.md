# Analytics and BI

* [Analytics and BI](analytics-bi.md) - Help people explore, visualise and report on data to make decisions.
  * [Ad hoc analysis](ad-hoc-analysis.md) - Explore data freely with SQL editors, pivots and visual exploration to answer one-off questions.
  * [Alerts and subscriptions](data-alerting.md) - Notify people when data crosses thresholds, or deliver reports to them on a schedule.
  * [Analytical sandboxes](analytical-sandboxes.md) - Give analysts and teams governed spaces on the platform where they can upload files, create tables and try out analyses, with quotas, expiry and the platform's access controls.
  * [Augmented analytics](augmented-analytics.md) - Use automation and machine learning to surface insights, anomalies and explanations without being asked.
  * [Conversational analytics](conversational-analytics.md) - Ask questions of data in natural language and get answers, charts or SQL back.
  * [Dashboards and interactive reports](dashboards-reports.md) - Build and share visual, interactive dashboards with filters and drill-down.
    * [Dashboards as code](dashboards-as-code.md) - Define dashboards in version-controlled files that are reviewed and tested alongside the models they use.
  * [Data consumer portal](data-consumer-portal.md) - Give business users one place to find the dashboards, apps and AI agents shared with them, without engineering tools.
  * [Data entry and write-back](data-writeback.md) - Let users enter or correct data in analytics tools and write it back to governed tables with an audit trail.
    * [Approved manual adjustments](manual-adjustments.md) - Record manual adjustments to reported figures with approval steps, reasons and a full audit trail.
  * [Embedded analytics](embedded-analytics.md) - Put dashboards and analytics inside other applications and portals, often for external customers.
  * [Experimentation and causal measurement](experimentation.md) - Run controlled experiments with holdout groups and measure whether an action caused the outcome.
  * [Packaged analytics content](packaged-analytics.md) - Use prebuilt models, metrics and dashboards for a business domain that a vendor delivers and updates.
  * [Paginated reports](paginated-reports.md) - Produce formatted, printable reports with precise layout for operational and regulatory use.
  * [Planning and forecasting](enterprise-planning.md) - Build budgets, forecasts and plans over governed data, with versions, allocations and approval workflows.
  * [Semantic layer](semantic-layer.md) - Define business entities, measures and relationships once so every tool calculates them the same way.
    * [Governed metric definitions](metric-definitions.md) - Define, certify and version key business metrics with owners and calculation logic.
    * [Semantic model interchange](semantic-model-interchange.md) - Exchange semantic models and metric definitions between BI tools, semantic layers and AI tools in a standard format.
  * [Spreadsheet integration](spreadsheet-integration.md) - Work with governed platform data directly from spreadsheet tools.
