# Data quality and observability

* [Data quality and observability](data-quality.md) - Measure, monitor and improve whether data is fit for its intended use.
  * [Bad-data quarantine](bad-data-quarantine.md) - Route records that fail checks to a holding area for inspection and replay instead of failing or loading them.
  * [Data cleansing and standardisation](data-cleansing.md) - Correct and standardise data values such as addresses, names, dates and codes.
  * [Data issue management](data-issue-management.md) - Log, assign, track and resolve data quality incidents with their owners.
  * [Data observability](data-observability.md) - Monitor data in production for freshness, volume, schema and distribution problems, and alert owners.
    * [Data anomaly detection](data-anomaly-detection.md) - Learn normal patterns in data and alert on unexpected changes without hand-written rules.
    * [Freshness monitoring](freshness-monitoring.md) - Detect when data arrives later than expected or misses its freshness target.
  * [Data profiling](data-profiling.md) - Analyse data to understand its structure, distributions, completeness and anomalies.
  * [Data quality rules](data-quality-rules.md) - Define and run checks that data must pass, such as completeness, validity, uniqueness and consistency.
  * [Data quality scorecards](data-quality-scorecards.md) - Track quality results over time and report them by dataset, domain and quality dimension.
  * [Data reconciliation](data-reconciliation.md) - Prove that data moved between systems is complete and correct using counts, totals and record matching.
  * [Unstructured data quality](unstructured-data-quality.md) - Assess document collections for duplication, staleness, sensitive content and fitness for retrieval or training.
