# Governance and compliance

* [Governance and compliance](governance.md) - Set and enforce the rules, roles and controls for how data is managed and used.
  * [AI governance](ai-governance.md) - Inventory AI systems and govern their risk, approval, documentation and use in line with regulation.
    * [AI impact assessment](ai-impact-assessment.md) - Assess and record an AI system's effects on people and society before release and whenever it changes.
    * [AI incident management](ai-incident-management.md) - Record, investigate and report incidents and malfunctions involving AI systems, including notifying regulators where required.
    * [AI system discovery](ai-system-discovery.md) - Find the AI models, agents and services in use, including unapproved ones, to build and maintain the AI inventory.
    * [Human oversight of AI](human-oversight.md) - Route model and agent decisions to people for review, approval or override, and let them pause or stop a system.
  * [Compliance controls and evidence](compliance-evidence.md) - Map regulatory requirements to platform controls and produce evidence for auditors.
  * [Critical data elements](critical-data-elements.md) - Identify the data items that matter most for regulatory, risk and financial reporting and apply stricter controls to them.
  * [Data classification](data-classification.md) - Label data by sensitivity and type, such as personal, confidential or regulated, manually or automatically.
  * [Data domains](data-domains.md) - Group data assets, products and owners into business domains with their own stewardship and policies.
  * [Data licensing and usage rights](data-usage-rights.md) - Record licence terms and permitted uses of datasets and check use against them, including for model training.
  * [Data residency and sovereignty](data-residency.md) - Keep data and its processing in approved jurisdictions and prove that you have.
  * [Governance coverage metrics](governance-metrics.md) - Measure how much of the estate is owned, described, classified, monitored and access-controlled, and track improvement.
  * [Ownership and stewardship](data-ownership.md) - Assign accountable owners and stewards to data and record their responsibilities.
  * [Policy management](data-policy-management.md) - Define data policies centrally and apply them consistently across platforms and tools.
  * [Privacy management](privacy-management.md) - Meet privacy obligations such as purpose limitation, consent and individuals' rights over their data.
    * [Consent and purpose management](consent-management.md) - Record what each person agreed to and restrict use of their data to permitted purposes.
    * [Data subject requests](data-subject-requests.md) - Find, export or erase a person's data across the platform when they ask.
    * [De-identification](deidentification.md) - Pseudonymise, tokenise or anonymise data so it can be used with less privacy risk.
    * [Privacy impact assessment](privacy-impact-assessment.md) - Trigger, carry out and record data protection impact assessments for high-risk processing, linked to the data and pipelines concerned.
    * [Privacy-enhancing technologies](privacy-enhancing-technologies.md) - Analyse data while limiting what is revealed, using techniques such as differential privacy, aggregation thresholds and secure computation.
    * [Records of processing](records-of-processing.md) - Keep a register of personal data processing with purposes, categories, recipients and retention, linked to the data involved.
  * [Retention and disposal](data-retention.md) - Keep data for as long as required, then delete it reliably, including from history, snapshots and backups, with legal holds when needed.
  * [Stewardship workflows](stewardship-workflows.md) - Route governance tasks such as term approvals, classification reviews, access approvals and match reviews to the right steward, with queues, deadlines, delegation and a record of each decision.
