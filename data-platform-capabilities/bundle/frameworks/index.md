# Frameworks, standards and regulations

* [BCBS 239](framework-bcbs-239.md) - Fourteen principles for how banks aggregate risk data and produce risk reports, in normal times and under stress.
* [CDMC (Cloud Data Management Capabilities)](framework-edm-cdmc.md) - A framework of fourteen key controls for managing sensitive data in cloud and hybrid environments, with certification for cloud platforms and tools.
* [DAMA-DMBOK 2](framework-dama-dmbok.md) - The Data Management Body of Knowledge, a reference guide that splits data management into knowledge areas around a central governance function.
* [Data mesh principles](framework-data-mesh.md) - Four principles for decentralising analytical data ownership to business domains while keeping data interoperable and governed.
* [DCAM v3](framework-edm-dcam.md) - The Data Management Capability Assessment Model, an evidence-based framework for scoring how mature an organisation's data management programme is.
* [DORA (Digital Operational Resilience Act)](framework-eu-dora.md) - Regulation (EU) 2022/2554, which sets ICT risk, incident, resilience testing and third-party requirements for EU financial entities.
* [EU AI Act](framework-eu-ai-act.md) - Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, which sets risk-based rules for placing AI systems and general-purpose AI models on the EU market and using them.
* [EU Data Act](framework-eu-data-act.md) - Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 on fair access to and use of data, covering connected-product data, business-to-government access and switching between cloud services.
* [GDPR](framework-gdpr.md) - Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the EU's general law on processing personal data, which sets principles, legal bases, individuals' rights and controller duties.
* [Google Cloud MLOps levels](framework-google-mlops-levels.md) - Google Cloud's three-level description of how far an organisation has automated the building, training and release of machine learning systems.
* [ISO 8000 (data quality)](framework-iso-8000.md) - A series of international standards on data quality, covering concepts, quality management processes, profiling, rules, master data exchange and sensor data.
* [ISO/IEC 42001:2023](framework-iso-iec-42001.md) - The international standard for an AI management system, setting requirements for governing AI responsibly within an organisation.
* [Microsoft MLOps maturity model](framework-microsoft-mlops-maturity.md) - A five-level model from Microsoft that describes MLOps maturity across people, model creation, model release and application integration.
* [NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)](framework-nist-ai-rmf.md) - A voluntary framework for managing AI risk through four functions, Govern, Map, Measure and Manage, broken into categories and subcategories of outcomes.
* [Open Data Contract Standard (ODCS)](framework-bitol-odcs.md) - An open YAML standard for describing a data contract, covering schema, quality, service levels, roles, pricing and where the data is served.
* [Open Data Product Standard (ODPS)](framework-bitol-odps.md) - An open YAML standard for describing a data product, its input and output ports, management interfaces, team and support.
