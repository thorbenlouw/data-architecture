# Maturity

* [Maturity scale](maturity-scale.md) - The five maturity levels used by every maturity track and every use case requirement in the bundle.

# Tracks

* [AI and model governance](track-ai-governance.md) - How well the organisation knows its AI systems, assesses their risks and impacts, controls their behaviour and evidences this to regulators and customers.
* [Analytics and BI](track-analytics-bi.md) - How consistently people across the organisation explore, report on and act on data using shared definitions and governed tools.
* [Compute and workload management](track-compute.md) - How well compute is provisioned, isolated, scaled and tuned so that workloads meet performance targets at a known cost.
* [Data products](track-data-products.md) - How far data is managed as products with accountable owners, known consumers, explicit contracts and service levels.
* [Data quality and observability](track-data-quality.md) - How systematically the organisation defines, measures, monitors and fixes the fitness of data for its intended uses.
* [Data serving](track-serving.md) - How well curated data reaches applications, people and operational tools at the latency, volume and reliability they need.
* [Data sharing](track-data-sharing.md) - How safely and efficiently data is shared between teams and with external parties without unnecessary copies.
* [FinOps](track-finops.md) - How well the organisation understands, allocates and controls what the data platform costs, relative to the value it delivers.
* [Generative AI](track-genai.md) - How safely and reliably the organisation builds, evaluates and runs applications that use language models and agents with its own data.
* [Governance operating model](track-governance.md) - How clearly the rules, roles and controls for managing data are defined, implemented on the platform and evidenced.
* [Ingestion and integration](track-ingestion.md) - How reliably, quickly and safely the organisation brings data from source systems into the platform and keeps it in step with them.
* [Lineage](track-lineage.md) - How completely and automatically the organisation can show where data came from, how it was changed and what depends on it.
* [Master and reference data](track-master-data.md) - How consistently core business entities and shared code lists are identified, matched and maintained across systems.
* [Metadata and catalogue](track-metadata-catalogue.md) - How completely and accurately data assets are described, findable and connected through metadata, for people and for machines.
* [ML development](track-ml-development.md) - How repeatably the organisation turns data into trained, evaluated and documented models.
* [MLOps](track-mlops.md) - How automatically and safely models are released, run, monitored and improved in production.
* [Orchestration](track-orchestration.md) - How dependably the organisation schedules, sequences and recovers the jobs that move and transform data.
* [Path to live](track-path-to-live.md) - How safely, quickly and repeatably changes to data and AI workloads move from a developer's first commit to production.
* [Platform migration and portability](track-platform-migration.md) - How safely and predictably the organisation moves workloads, code and data to new platforms, and how freely it can choose where the platform runs.
* [Platform self-service](track-platform-self-service.md) - How far teams can obtain, run and observe what they need on the shared platform without waiting for a central team.
* [Privacy](track-privacy.md) - How reliably the organisation meets its obligations to the people its data describes, from purpose and consent to rights requests and de-identification.
* [Real-time and streaming](track-real-time.md) - How well the organisation moves, processes and queries data continuously, with predictable latency and correct results, instead of in scheduled batches.
* [Resilience and recovery](track-resilience.md) - How well the platform withstands and recovers from data loss, corruption, ransomware and outages within agreed targets.
* [Security and access control](track-security.md) - How well data is protected from unauthorised access, loss and misuse, from identity through fine-grained access to threat detection.
* [Specialised data](track-specialised-data.md) - How well the organisation stores, governs and analyses data that needs specialised types, indexes or engines, such as time series, location, graphs, searchable text, media, logs and bitemporal history.
* [Storage and table management](track-storage.md) - How well data is kept in durable, open, maintained tables that many engines can read, update and trust.
* [Transformation engineering](track-transformation.md) - How consistently transformation logic is written, tested, reused and run efficiently, from raw data to tables ready for use.
