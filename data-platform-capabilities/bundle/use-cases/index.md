# Use cases

* [Agentic process automation](usecase-agentic-automation.md) - AI agents complete multi-step business tasks using enterprise data and tools under controls.
* [Application data publishing](usecase-application-data-publishing.md) - Application teams publish their data to the platform through versioned events and change capture under agreed contracts, so downstream consumers do not break when applications change.
* [Churn prediction](usecase-churn-prediction.md) - Score customers regularly by likelihood to leave so teams can act before they do.
* [Conversational analytics for business users](usecase-conversational-bi.md) - Business users ask questions of governed data in plain language and get trustworthy answers.
* [Credit decisioning](usecase-credit-decisioning.md) - Score credit applications and decide to approve, decline or refer them, with reasons, fairness checks and model risk controls.
* [Customer 360](usecase-customer-360.md) - Give every channel a single, trusted view of each customer.
* [Customer-facing analytics](usecase-customer-facing-analytics.md) - Give external customers fast, secure analytics inside a product.
* [Data sharing and monetisation](usecase-data-monetisation.md) - Share or sell data products to partners and customers under agreed terms.
* [Demand forecasting](usecase-demand-forecasting.md) - Forecast demand by product, location and time to plan stock, staff and supply.
* [Document data extraction](usecase-document-extraction.md) - Extract fields and tables from invoices, claims, contracts and forms into business systems, with people reviewing uncertain results.
* [Enterprise management reporting](usecase-enterprise-reporting.md) - Produce trusted, consistent KPIs and management reports across the organisation.
* [IoT predictive maintenance](usecase-iot-predictive-maintenance.md) - Analyse sensor telemetry to predict equipment failures before they happen.
* [Knowledge assistant](usecase-rag-assistant.md) - A generative AI assistant that answers questions from internal documents, with citations.
* [Legacy warehouse migration](usecase-platform-modernisation.md) - Move from an on-premises warehouse or Hadoop estate to a cloud data platform.
* [Marketing audience activation](usecase-marketing-activation.md) - Build audiences from platform data and sync them to marketing and advertising tools.
* [Operational reporting](usecase-operational-reporting.md) - Report on current operations within minutes of events happening.
* [Personalised recommendations](usecase-recommendations.md) - Recommend products or content to each user in real time.
* [Platform cost management and chargeback](usecase-platform-cost-management.md) - Attribute, forecast and control what the data platform costs, and recover it from the teams and products that use it.
* [Privacy rights handling](usecase-privacy-rights.md) - Find, report and erase an individual's data across the estate within legal deadlines.
* [Real-time fraud detection](usecase-fraud-detection.md) - Score transactions in milliseconds and block or flag suspicious ones.
* [Regulatory reporting](usecase-regulatory-reporting.md) - Produce accurate, auditable and reproducible submissions to regulators.
* [Security and log analytics](usecase-security-log-analytics.md) - Collect and analyse large volumes of logs to detect threats and investigate incidents.
* [Self-service BI](usecase-self-service-bi.md) - Analysts and business users explore governed data and build their own reports with trusted metrics.
* [Self-service domain onboarding](usecase-domain-onboarding.md) - Give a new domain or product team a governed workspace, path to live and budget on the shared platform within a day.
* [Sensitive data protection](usecase-sensitive-data-protection.md) - Know where sensitive and personal data is across the platform, control who can reach it through one set of policies, and prove it to auditors.
