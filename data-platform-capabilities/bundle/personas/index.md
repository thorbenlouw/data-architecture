# Personas

* [AI engineer](persona-ai-engineer.md) - Builds applications and agents on foundation models and enterprise data.
* [Analytics engineer](persona-analytics-engineer.md) - Turns raw data into tested, documented, analysis-ready models.
* [Business user](persona-business-user.md) - Uses data, reports and AI assistants to make decisions, without deep technical skills.
* [Chief data officer](persona-cdo.md) - Accountable for the organisation's data strategy, value, risk and governance.
* [Data analyst](persona-data-analyst.md) - Answers business questions through analysis, dashboards and reports.
* [Data architect](persona-data-architect.md) - Designs the data platform and how data flows, is modelled and is governed across the organisation.
* [Data engineer](persona-data-engineer.md) - Builds and operates the pipelines that ingest, transform and deliver data.
* [Data platform engineer](persona-platform-engineer.md) - Builds and runs the shared data platform and its self-service tooling.
* [Data product owner](persona-data-product-owner.md) - Owns the value, roadmap and consumers of one or more data products.
* [Data scientist](persona-data-scientist.md) - Explores data and builds statistical and machine learning models.
* [Data steward](persona-data-steward.md) - Looks after the definition, quality and proper use of data in a business domain.
* [FinOps practitioner](persona-finops-practitioner.md) - Tracks, allocates and optimises what the data platform costs.
* [ML engineer](persona-ml-engineer.md) - Takes models to production and keeps them running reliably.
* [Risk and compliance officer](persona-risk-compliance.md) - Needs assurance, with evidence, that data controls meet regulatory and internal requirements.
* [Security and privacy officer](persona-security-officer.md) - Protects data and makes sure it is used lawfully.
* [Software engineer](persona-software-engineer.md) - Builds applications that produce data for the platform or consume data from it.
