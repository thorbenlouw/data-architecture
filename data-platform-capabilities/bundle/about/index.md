# About this bundle

* [Using this bundle](using-this-bundle.md) - What the bundle contains, how it is organised and how to answer the common questions with it.
* [Conventions](conventions.md) - The frontmatter fields, relationship rules, naming rules and editorial rules every concept follows.
* [Sources and method](sources-and-method.md) - How the first version of the bundle was researched and written, how it was checked, and its known limitations.
* [Choosing between similar capabilities](similar-capabilities.md) - Groups of capabilities that are easily confused, with one line on what each is and when to choose it.
* [Read paths for applications](application-read-paths.md) - How an application should read data from the platform, or publish data to it, compared by latency, concurrency, freshness, consistency and coupling.
* [Mapping a use case to capabilities](mapping-a-use-case.md) - A practical method for turning a business use case into required capabilities with minimum maturity levels, checking platform fit and recording the result.
* [Running a maturity assessment](assessing-maturity.md) - How to assess maturity with the tracks, from scope and evidence to scoring, gap analysis against use cases, presentation and re-assessment.
